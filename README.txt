HeFeMi single-page website
- Open index.html in a browser to preview.
- Booking form includes name, phone, hotel, room, date, duration and time choice.
- Session prices: 1 hour ₹500; 2 hours ₹900. No extra convenience fee.
- On submit, the form opens WhatsApp chat to +91 82877 61407 with the booking details pre-filled.
- The customer must press Send in WhatsApp. A booking is not confirmed until the team replies.
- The form is front-end only; it does not store bookings or enforce shared live availability / 40-minute gaps between different customers.
- All website files are at the ZIP root when extracted (index.html and README.txt).
